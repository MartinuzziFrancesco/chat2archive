# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include
demonstrating empathy, being respectful of differing opinions, giving and
gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for
the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, publishing others' private information without
permission, and other conduct which could reasonably be considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainers by opening a confidential issue or contacting
them directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly.

For the full text of the Contributor Covenant, see
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
