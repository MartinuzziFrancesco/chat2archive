User: What's a good first step for archiving an AI conversation?

Assistant: Start by exporting the conversation from the provider in its
official format, since that gives the strongest capture provenance.

User: And if I don't have an export, just a copy-pasted chat log?

Assistant: That still works, but it is recorded as the weakest capture
provenance class, since message boundaries and roles have to be inferred
from plain text rather than observed directly.
